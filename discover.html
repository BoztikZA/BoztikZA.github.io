A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Before you choose.

Does Boztik recommend only paid products?

No. The directory includes Boztik's own free tools and learning resources alongside selected third-party services. A paid subscription is not automatically the best choice for every creator.

How are resources selected?

We prioritise relevance to real creative workflows, a clear purpose, useful official product information and transparent terms. Recommendations are editorial selections, not a guarantee that a product will suit every person or project.

Does Boztik currently earn commission from these links?

No. The external Adobe and Envato links currently shown on this page are standard links and do not generate commission for Boztik. If a link becomes an approved affiliate link in future, it will be clearly identified and this disclosure will be updated.

Can product features, pricing or licences change?

Yes. Third-party products and plans can change. Always check the provider's official website and current licence, pricing, billing and cancellation terms before making a decision.
