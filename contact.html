Help me understand the picture.

You don't need a perfect brief. A few helpful details can make it much easier to understand what you need and point you in the right direction.

  • ✓ A clear description of what you'd like help with
  • ✓ Your preferred outcome or any important deadline
  • ✓ Reference images, screenshots or links, if relevant

BEFORE YOU WRITE

A few useful answers.

Quick answers to common questions about getting in touch.

How quickly will I receive a reply?

I'll respond as soon as reasonably possible. Boztik is independently run, so response times can vary depending on workload and the type of enquiry. I don't promise an instant or fixed turnaround.

Can I request a quote for photo editing?

Yes. Send a description of the work and, where appropriate, a reference image. The complexity, condition of the original and desired result all help determine what may be involved. I'll discuss the scope with you before any work is agreed.

What should I include when reporting a technical problem?

Include the name of the tool or page, the device and browser you're using, what you expected to happen, what happened instead, and the steps to reproduce it. A screenshot can help, but remove private information first.

Do I need to know exactly what to ask for?

No. Explain the situation in your own words. If you're unsure which service or tool is right, give me enough context to understand the goal and we can take it from there.
