Boztik Creative Toolkit Privacy Policy

Boztik Creative Toolkit respects your privacy. This policy explains how the extension and website handle information, permissions, and external services.

Last updated: August 1, 2026 Back to Home

At a glance

Boztik Creative Toolkit does not require an account and does not collect personal profile data. The extension is designed to process image analysis locally in the browser whenever possible and to leave your device data on your device.

  • No personal information is collected, transmitted, sold, rented, or shared.
  • Extension preferences and settings are stored locally in the browser.
  • Image analysis is performed locally whenever possible.
  • Third-party services remain subject to their own privacy policies.

Information collection

Boztik Creative Toolkit does not collect personal information by default. There is no account creation requirement, and no personal profile information is gathered for marketing or advertising purposes.

Local storage and settings

Any extension settings or preferences that are saved are stored locally using the browser storage API. They are not transmitted to Boztik servers and remain on the device unless the user clears them.

Image processing

Image analysis and inspection are handled locally in the browser whenever possible. Uploaded or viewed images are not collected or stored by Boztik Creative Toolkit as a central service.

Data sharing and security

User data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising purposes. Reasonable browser-side security practices are used to protect locally stored settings and preferences.

Permissions

Permissions are requested only to support the extension’s intended functionality. Storage saves preferences locally, downloads allows the download-original feature, active tab and host permissions support image analysis and context-aware browsing features.

Third-party services

If a user chooses to open services such as Google Reverse Image Search, Bing Visual Search, TinEye, or Ko-fi, they are redirected to those services and become subject to each provider’s own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

Boztik Creative Toolkit is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian becomes aware of a child’s information being provided without consent, please contact us so it can be removed.


Policy updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or improved privacy practices. Any changes will be reflected on this page.


Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be directed to hello@boztik.dev. You may also visit the Boztik website and use the support links available there.

Need help or have questions?

We’re happy to answer questions about privacy, extension permissions, or how Boztik Creative Toolkit operates.
