Privacy Policy

We believe your privacy matters. This policy explains how Boztik Creative Toolkit handles information, browser permissions, and your privacy while using our extension and website.

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

Boztik Creative Toolkit is designed with privacy in mind.

The extension does not collect, transmit, sell, or store personal information on our servers.

  • We do not require users to create an account.
  • We do not track browsing activity.
  • We do not build user profiles.

2. Local Storage

Some extension settings, preferences, statistics, and achievements are stored locally on your own device using the browser Storage API.

This information never leaves your computer unless you explicitly choose to share it.

3. Image Processing

Image analysis is performed locally within your browser whenever possible.

Boztik Creative Toolkit does not upload or permanently store images that you inspect.

4. Browser Permissions

Browser permissions are requested only to support the extension’s intended functionality.

Storage

Used only to save your preferences and extension settings locally.

Downloads

Used only when you choose to download an image using the Download Original feature.

Host Permissions

Required so the extension can inspect images available on websites that you choose to interact with.

Permissions are never used to collect personal information.

5. Third-Party Services

Users may voluntarily open services such as Google Reverse Image Search, Bing Visual Search, TinEye, or Ko-fi.

Once users visit those services, they are subject to those companies’ own Privacy Policies. Boztik Creative Toolkit does not control those websites.

6. Data Sharing

We never sell, rent, or share user information with advertisers or third parties.

7. Data Security

We use privacy-first design principles.

No personal information is transmitted to Boztik servers. Locally stored information remains on the user’s own device.

8. Children’s Privacy

Boztik Creative Toolkit is not intended for children under 13 years of age and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements, legal requirements, or changes to the extension.

The latest version will always be published on this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Boztik website or our published support email.

For direct contact, email hello@boztik.dev.
