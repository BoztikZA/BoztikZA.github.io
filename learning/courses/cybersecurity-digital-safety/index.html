Cybersecurity & Digital Safety Fundamentals

Protect your accounts, devices and information from the scams and threats ordinary people actually face.

FreeBeginner–Intermediate5–6 hours10 modules · 29 lessons
See the syllabus

About this course

This course teaches the habits that stop most everyday cyber harm: strong unique passwords, multi-factor authentication, spotting scams, keeping good backups and sharing less. Every module ends with something you do on your own accounts and devices, and the final module turns it all into a prioritised 30-day plan.

Before you start: You should be comfortable using a smartphone or computer, email and a web browser; no technical or security background is needed.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain confidentiality, integrity and availability and use them to decide what you need to protect
  • Create long, unique passwords and manage them with a password manager
  • Set up multi-factor authentication sensibly and recover access if you lose your phone
  • Recognise phishing and common scams across email, SMS, WhatsApp, phone calls and QR codes
  • Reduce the damage malware and ransomware can do with updates and tested backups
  • Browse, download and install software without falling for fake buttons, updates and warnings
  • Protect personal and business information, and secure your home router and mobile devices
  • Carry out a personal security assessment and follow a prioritised 30-day action plan

Who it is for

  • Anyone who banks, shops, studies or chats online and wants to feel safer doing it
  • Freelancers, side-hustlers and one-person businesses who keep customer details on their own devices
  • Parents and family members who are the unofficial IT support for everyone else
  • Beginners who have heard the jargon and want it explained plainly, and intermediate users who want a structured plan
