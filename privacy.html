Boztik Creative Toolkit Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Boztik Creative Toolkit collects, how it is handled, and how the extension uses browser permissions.

Overview

Boztik Creative Toolkit is a browser extension and website designed to help users inspect images, extract colors, and get creative assistance. The extension is built to prioritize privacy and local processing wherever possible.


What data is collected

Boztik does not require account creation and does not collect personal profile data. The extension does not upload your images to Boztik servers by default. Images you analyze are processed locally in your browser. If you choose to connect a third-party AI provider, your API key is stored only in your browser's local storage and is sent directly to the provider you selected.


How data is handled

Any image analysis performed on the site is handled locally in the browser. The site may temporarily access image data for display and analysis while you are using the tool. No personal data is sold or shared for advertising purposes. The only data Boztik may receive from a user interaction is general usage information that is not personally identifying, such as anonymous error reports or basic analytics that may be enabled by the hosting provider.


How data is stored

Local browser data, such as a saved API key, is stored in your browser using local storage. This data remains on your device unless you remove it or clear browser storage. Boztik does not maintain a central database of user images or personal content.


Do we share data

Boztik does not share personal data with third parties for marketing or resale. If you choose to use an external AI provider, the request is sent directly from your browser to that provider using the API key you provide. Boztik does not receive the contents of those requests unless the provider returns a response to your browser.


Third-party services

Boztik may use third-party services for hosting, fonts, analytics, and optional AI provider requests. These may include Google Fonts, Google AdSense, and the APIs of AI providers you choose to connect to. Their own privacy practices apply to those requests.


Why extension permissions are required

Permissions are used only to support the intended feature set of the extension. They may be requested to access tabs, active page context, or selected content so the extension can analyze images or provide creative assistance while you browse. Boztik does not use these permissions for unrelated tracking or surveillance.


Security practices

Boztik uses standard web security practices for the public website and browser extension experience. However, no online system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices and browser profile with strong passwords and up-to-date browser security settings.


Children's privacy

This service is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that a child has provided personal information without consent, please contact us so it can be removed.


Policy updates

This policy may be updated from time to time to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or improved privacy practices. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date.


Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be sent to hello@boztik.dev.
