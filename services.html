Photo Editing Services

Professional photo editing, done by hand.

Restoration, retouching, and enhancement work for personal photographs, portraits, and business images — edited individually, not run through a filter.

Photo Restoration

Repair for old, damaged, faded, or torn photographs — prints and scanned negatives both work. Scratches, dust, creases, water damage, missing sections, and faded color or tone are addressed individually rather than with a single blanket filter.

  • Scratch, dust, and tear repair
  • Fading and tonal reconstruction
  • Missing detail and facial reconstruction
  • Black-and-white colorization
  • Print-ready output at your target size

Portrait & Headshots

Retouching for personal portraits and professional headshots — skin, lighting, and background cleanup that still looks like a real photograph of a real person, not a plastic over-edit.

  • Natural skin retouching
  • Background cleanup or replacement
  • Color and lighting correction
  • LinkedIn / professional-profile formatting

Object Removal

Removing distracting people, objects, wires, blemishes on surfaces, or backgrounds elements — with the surrounding area reconstructed so the removal doesn't read as an edit.

  • Unwanted people or objects removed
  • Background distractions cleaned up
  • Texture and pattern reconstruction behind removed areas

Image Enhancement

General improvement for photos that are blurry, low-resolution, poorly lit, or noisy — sharpening, upscaling, and correction that stays within what the source detail can support.

  • Low-resolution upscaling
  • Sharpening and noise reduction
  • Exposure and color correction
  • Preparation for print or social media
