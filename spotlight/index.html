A DIRECTORY FOR INDEPENDENT WORK

Meet the people behind independent businesses and creative projects.

Boztik Spotlight brings together small businesses, independent creators and freelancers in one place. Browse approved profiles to understand what each person offers, explore their work and follow the links they provide to their own websites and channels.

Small businesses

Discover independent shops, specialist services and small teams offering products or services directly to their customers.

Creators and freelancers

Explore the work of video creators, streamers, photographers, designers, musicians and other independent professionals.

Creative and digital work

Browse profiles connected to photography, design, gaming, music, online content and other creative interests.

How Spotlight works: applications are reviewed before publication. Approved directory profiles remain listed for 30 days and enter the rotating pool for the homepage feature. One profile is showcased at a time, with selection and timing curated by Boztik. A listing is an opportunity to be discovered, not a promise of traffic, followers or sales.
