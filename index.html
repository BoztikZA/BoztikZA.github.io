Photos restored. Creative tools built. Skills shared clearly.

Boztik is an independent creative studio focused on thoughtful photo restoration, practical image-editing services, free browser tools and straightforward tutorials. We also make original designs, available separately through Boztik Stores.

It started with the edit. The rest grew out of it.

Boztik began with hands-on photo editing and restoration. The browser tools came from the problems that work kept throwing up, and the original designs now sit beside it under the same name.

  • Photo editing and restoration

    Restoration, retouching, object removal and compositing for personal and business images.

  • Free image tools

    An Image Inspector and a Creative Assistant that run in your browser, plus the Creative Toolkit extension. No account needed.

  • Original designs

    Merchandise on Redbubble and TeePublic, and wallpaper packs on Ko-fi.
