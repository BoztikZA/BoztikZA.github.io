Good to know

What is not tracked

Boztik records simple counters: how many times each delivery was opened or downloaded, per day, and how many times each public page was loaded. It does not record visitor location, device, browser or referring site, and it stores no IP addresses or cookies for analytics. That is deliberate — it also means there is no location or device breakdown to show here, and any such chart would have to be invented.
