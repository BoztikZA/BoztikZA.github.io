What is it?

Your image toolbox, inside the browser.

Boztik Creative Toolkit is a browser extension for Chrome and Edge that brings useful image and creative tools together in one workspace.

Instead of opening a separate site to check an image's size, another to find its source and another to resize it, you find the image on any webpage and open it in Image Inspector. From there you can check its quality, look for the original, pull out its colours, resize or convert a copy, or look up the fonts on the page.

It is free to use and doesn't need an account.

Graphic designers

Check that a supplied logo is big enough for a layout, and pull its colours.

Photographers

Shrink and convert images for a post before you upload them.

    Browse as normal. Any webpage that shows images works.

Privacy & control

You decide where it works and what it connects to.

Plain statements about what stays on your device and what doesn't.

Opt-in website permissions

Website access is opt-in. The Toolkit works on a site once you allow it, and you can hide the floating button on any site. Your browser's extension settings also show and control site access.

No account required

There is no Boztik account or subscription. Optional AI keys are stored in your browser and can be disconnected or removed at any time.

Local processing where supported

Image inspection and capture tools process images in your browser and don't upload them to Boztik's servers. Image Lab processes locally where the tool supports it. Not every feature is local, as shown below.

Optional third-party AI

The AI Creative Assistant works with your own OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude) or Google (Gemini) API key. Requests go directly from your browser to the provider you choose. Boztik does not receive your key or AI conversations. The provider's terms and any API charges apply and are separate from Boztik.

Stays in your browser
  • Image inspection and capture
  • Palette and colour work
  • Image Lab, where the tool supports local processing
  • The built-in AI quick-reference guidance
Uses an outside service
  • Reverse image search, which relies on a search service
  • Provider-powered AI features, which call the AI provider you connect
