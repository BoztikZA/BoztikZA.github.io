HOW I CAN HELP

What does your photo need?

Choose the service closest to your goal. If you are unsure, send the image and explain what you would like to improve.

01BRING IT BACK

Photo restoration

Give damaged or ageing photographs a new lease on life while respecting the character of the original image.

  • Scratches, dust, creases and tears
  • Faded colour and uneven tones
  • Careful repair of damaged areas
  • Black-and-white photo colourization

Ideal for: family keepsakes, old prints and scanned photographs.

02KEEP IT YOU

Portraits & headshots

Polished portraits that still look like the person in the photograph. The goal is refinement, not an artificial makeover.

  • Natural skin retouching
  • Lighting and colour balancing
  • Background cleanup or replacement
  • Professional profile-image preparation

Ideal for: personal portraits, LinkedIn and business profiles.

03REMOVE DISTRACTIONS

Object removal

Clean up unwanted elements so the important subject gets the attention. Surrounding textures and details are rebuilt as naturally as possible.

  • Unwanted objects or people
  • Background distractions and clutter
  • Small blemishes and visual interruptions
  • Reconstruction of the affected area

Ideal for: travel photos, product images and cleaner compositions.

04MAKE IT CLEARER

Image enhancement

Improve the overall presentation of an image with balanced adjustments, while staying realistic about what the original file can support.

  • Exposure, contrast and colour correction
  • Noise reduction and sharpening
  • Upscaling where appropriate
  • Preparation for print or digital use

Ideal for: low-quality images, social content and print preparation.

Every image is different. The achievable result depends on the original file, image quality and complexity of the requested work. I’ll explain what is realistic before proceeding.

Send the highest-quality file you have. Avoid screenshots or files that have been repeatedly compressed if the original is available.
