A LITTLE ABOUT MY WORK

One creative practice.
Two ways to help.

I believe good creative work should feel considered, useful and genuinely human.

My work combines hands-on photo editing with building browser-based tools. Editing taught me how much the small details matter — preserving a person’s likeness, rebuilding a damaged area carefully, or making an improvement without making the photograph feel artificial.

That same practical mindset shapes the tools I build. Boztik is an independent project, developed around real creative workflows and the everyday tasks that can slow them down. The aim is simple: deliver careful editing, make useful resources accessible, and keep improving both.

THE APPROACH

Better results.
Never at the cost of realism.

01

Respect the original

Preserve identity, important details and the character of the photograph. Improvements should not erase what makes an image meaningful.

02

Work with intention

Every adjustment should have a reason. No unnecessary effects, heavy-handed retouching or one-click fixes where careful work is needed.

03

Make technology useful

Build tools around real tasks, keep interfaces understandable, and make privacy and reliability part of the experience.
