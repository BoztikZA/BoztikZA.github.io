Privacy and private links

The short version of what Boztik Learning stores, why, and what you control.

Private links (no password, no account)

When you enrol we create a random private link. We store only a hash of it, never the link itself. The link lets you carry on from any device. Anyone who has your link can open your learning session — treat it like a key. Course-sharing links never contain it.

You also get a one-time recovery code. With your reference and that code you can get a new private link; the old one then stops working. If you lose both, we cannot recover your session, because we have no email-based recovery.

What we store

  • Your full name (required, printed on your certificate)
  • Your email and country, only if you provide them
  • Which lessons you completed, your assessment attempts and scores, and your certificates
  • For optional contributions: the amount, status and PayPal order reference. PayPal hosts checkout; we never see or store card details

Certificate verification

A certificate can be checked by its ID. By default the verification page confirms the course and completion date only. It shows your name and score only if you tick “show my details”, and you can change that later. A person holding your certificate can type the name printed on it to confirm it matches, without the page revealing a name it was not given.

Your choices

From your dashboard you can delete your session and data at any time, optionally including your certificates. Certificates you keep remain verifiable by ID but stop showing your name publicly.

Analytics

The Learning pages do not load advertising scripts or Google Analytics. The course catalogue, course overview pages and public learning resource articles count page views with the same first-party counter used across boztik.com: it stores an anonymous random session cookie, never your name or email, and sends nothing if Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control is on. Lessons, exams, your dashboard, private links and certificate pages are never counted. Lessons and progress use only what is needed to run the service.

Questions: contact Boztik. See also the site-wide Privacy Policy and Terms.
