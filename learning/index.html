How it works

Four steps, no account

  1. Pick a course

    Read the outline first. Enrol with your name — email and country are optional.

  2. Learn at your pace

    Short lessons with examples, exercises and knowledge checks. Progress saves on our server.

  3. Pass the assessment

    Marked on our server. Not passed yet? Review the lessons and retake it.

  4. Claim your certificate

    A print-ready PDF with a unique ID that anyone can verify.

Certificates

A certificate you actually earn

Pass the assessment and you can claim a Certificate of Achievement showing your name, the course, completion date, your score and the pass mark, with a unique ID and a public verification page.

  • Printed and downloadable as a PDF
  • Anyone can verify it by ID — and it shows only what you consent to share
  • Never locked behind a payment
Please read: Boztik courses are independently developed and not formally accredited. Certificates are certificates of completion or achievement, not academic qualifications or professional certifications.

Boztik Learning disclaimer

Boztik courses are independently developed educational and skills-development programmes designed to provide practical knowledge and learning opportunities.

These courses are not formally accredited by any recognised educational, professional or regulatory accreditation body.

Certificates issued by Boztik are certificates of completion or achievement for independently provided training. They do not represent formally recognised academic qualifications, professional certifications, academic credits or licences.

Completion of a Boztik course does not guarantee employment, professional recognition or acceptance by educational institutions or employers.

Our mission is to make practical, accessible and valuable learning opportunities available to everyone, everywhere.

Courses are free

Learn for free. Support if you like.

Every course, assessment and certificate is free. After you pass a course you will see an optional way to contribute — $5, $10, $15, $20 or an amount of your choice — to help maintain the Learning Hub and develop new courses. It is entirely voluntary, never required, and has no effect on your result or certificate. These are not charitable donations.

Good to know

Do I need an account?

No. You get a private link that saves your progress. Anyone with that link can open your session, so keep it safe. How private links work.

Can I learn on my phone?

Yes. Every page is built for phones first, and you can pick up on another device with your private link.

What if I fail the assessment?

Review the lessons and try again. Retakes are allowed, with a fresh question order each time.
