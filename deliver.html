Boztik Deliver

How finished work gets to you.

When a project is complete, you receive a private, secure link to download your files. No public gallery, no account needed — the link works on its own and expires automatically once your delivery window is over.

The delivery

Built for clients, not for show.

Deliver is the quiet part of working with Boztik — the part that gets your files back to you safely and then steps out of the way.

A private link for your project

Each completed job gets its own delivery link, unique to your files. The link is sent directly to you and is never listed or shared publicly.

View and download your files

Open the link to preview and download your images or files at the size they were finished. No sign-up, no upload — just your work.

Expires automatically

Deliveries are available for a set time after the project is shared, then the files are automatically removed. This keeps your work private and keeps storage clean.

What to expect

  • You'll receive a delivery link only after your project is finished.
  • The link is private to you — Boztik never publishes a public list of deliveries.
  • Downloads count toward your delivery window, and expired links are removed automatically.
  • If a link has expired, just get in touch and Boztik can re-upload the files for you.
Working with Boztik

Need editing or restoration work done?

Deliver is how finished Boztik work reaches clients. If you're looking to have work done, get in touch — and if you're here after a link expired, ask for a re-upload.
