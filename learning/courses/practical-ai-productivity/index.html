Practical Artificial Intelligence & AI Productivity

Use AI tools confidently, check what they give you, and build routines that save real time.

See the syllabus

About this course

Learn what AI really is, how chat assistants work, and how to write prompts that get useful results. You will practise research, writing, planning, small-business and image tasks, learn to protect private information and spot mistakes, and finish by designing an AI workflow for your own work. No technical background is needed, and every exercise can be done with a free tool or on paper.

Before you start: Basic comfort with a web browser and typing; a free AI chat tool is helpful but not required.

What you will be able to do

  • Explain in plain language what AI is, what it is not, and how language models produce answers
  • Choose the right kind of AI tool for a task and recognise when not to use AI at all
  • Write clear prompts using context, task, audience, format, constraints and examples, and improve them by iterating
  • Use AI to research, draft, edit, summarise and study while checking claims against real sources
  • Apply AI to email, meeting notes, planning and small-business tasks while keeping decisions and promises human
  • Describe images for AI tools and explain the honesty, copyright, likeness and photo-restoration risks
  • Protect personal, client and confidential information and write a personal AI-use policy
  • Design a reusable AI workflow with templates, a review checklist and a measure of time saved

Who it is for

  • Students and school-leavers who want to use AI honestly and effectively
  • Office workers and freelancers who want to save time on writing and admin
  • Small-business owners and side-hustlers handling customers, quotes and social posts
  • Anyone curious about AI who wants a practical, plain-language starting point

  • Read your school's, employer's or industry body's current guidance on AI use and keep your personal policy up to date.
  • Explore a free course on data basics or spreadsheets to check AI-generated numbers more confidently.
