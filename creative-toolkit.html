How It Works

From install to insight in three steps.

Install — free

Add the extension from the official Chrome Web Store or Microsoft Edge Add-ons. No account, no sign-up.

2

Pick a tool

Open any image, page, or asset and choose the tool you need — inspection, research, color, typography, or capture.

Privacy First

Your images and keys stay yours.

Local-first by design

Image analysis runs directly in your browser. Your images are not uploaded to Boztik's servers when you use the toolkit's inspection and capture tools.

Your AI, your key

The AI Creative Assistant is optional and connects to your own OpenAI, Anthropic (Claude), or Google (Gemini) API key. Requests go directly from your browser to the provider you choose — Boztik does not receive your key or AI conversations. Your provider's own terms and any API charges still apply.

You stay in control

Keys are stored only in your browser and can be disconnected or removed at any time. The toolkit is free to use and needs no account.

See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for full details on how the toolkit and website handle data.
