Two ways to use this page

Support or payment for agreed work

The same payment options serve different purposes. Choose the one that matches your situation.

Optional support

Enjoyed the work?

If Boztik helped with a free edit, or you simply enjoy the creative work, you can optionally support the project. Support is voluntary and is not payment for a commissioned edit.

Agreed creative work

Paying for an edit?

Use PayPal or Ko-fi to pay for an edit or service you have already agreed with Boztik. Payment is for that work, rather than an optional contribution.

For commissioned work

How your paid edit is delivered

Your image is delivered privately once payment has been confirmed and the work is ready.

  1. 1

    Pay

    Use PayPal or Ko-fi to complete payment for your agreed work.

  2. 2

    Payment is confirmed

    Boztik confirms the payment before the final delivery is released.

  3. 3

    Receive your finished image

    When the edit is ready, you’ll receive a private Boztik Deliver link to download it.

Payment does not automatically generate an instant download. Your private Boztik Deliver link is sent after payment has been confirmed and your edit is ready.
