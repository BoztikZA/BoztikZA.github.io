BOZTIK ORIGINAL · CREATIVE TOOLKIT

A capable creative workspace, right in your browser.

Useful tools. A focused workflow. No subscription required.

Boztik Creative Toolkit is our flagship browser extension, bringing image inspection and practical editing tools into a fast, focused workflow. Boztik Studio is the editor experience within the Toolkit, designed to make everyday creative work feel more approachable.

  • Image inspection and useful file details
  • Selection tools and object removal
  • Brushes for local refinements
  • Creative looks and image adjustments
  • Available for Chrome and Microsoft Edge
