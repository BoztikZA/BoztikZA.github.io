For commissioned work

How your paid edit is delivered

Your image is delivered privately once payment has been confirmed and the work is ready.

  1. 1

    Complete payment

    Pay using the provider we agreed on. If the checkout offers a note, include your Reddit username or the job reference we discussed.

  2. 2

    Payment is matched

    I confirm the payment and match it to the edit we agreed on. If anything is unclear, I’ll contact you before proceeding.

  3. 3

    Receive your finished work

    Once the edit is complete, I send you a private Boztik Deliver link so you can download the finished files.

Good to know: a payment does not trigger an instant download. Optional tips simply support the work. For a paid commission, I confirm the payment, complete the agreed edit and then send a private Boztik Deliver link. You can contact me if you need help with a payment or delivery.
