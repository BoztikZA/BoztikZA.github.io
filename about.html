About Us

Boztik Creative Toolkit

Built by creators, for creators. Professional tools designed with a focus on quality, usability, and genuine creative value.

What is Boztik?

Boztik Creative Toolkit is a professional browser extension designed for photographers, designers, digital artists, marketers, and content creators who need to work faster and make better creative decisions.

Our suite of tools includes the Image Inspector for deep image analysis, typography and font inspection, advanced color palette extraction, AI-powered creative assistance, and more — all built directly into your browser workflow. Instead of switching between tabs and applications, Boztik brings professional creative analysis to where you work.

The extension is free to use, automatically updated, and designed with privacy as a core principle. We don't track your browsing, store your data, or require account creation.

Who Built This?

Boztik is the work of an independent creator and digital artist focused on building tools that solve real problems in creative workflows. The approach is straightforward: build something useful, maintain it well, and keep it honest.

The creator has over a decade of experience in photography, image editing, design systems, and digital tooling. Boztik wasn't built to maximize engagement or collect data — it was built because the creator needed these tools and believed other creators would too.

Every feature, every line of documentation, and every design decision reflects a commitment to quality over growth. We maintain high standards for code quality, security, and user experience.

Our Mission

Boztik exists to help creators work more effectively and make more confident creative decisions. Our mission is simple:

  • Build tools that genuinely save time. Not features that look impressive in screenshots — tools that solve real workflow problems.
  • Maintain high quality standards. Every release is polished, tested, and built to last. We don't ship features that aren't ready.
  • Respect user privacy. Your data is yours. We process images locally in your browser and don't build tracking into our tools.
  • Create honest educational content. The guides on this site are written from real creative work, not from SEO templates or AI generation.
  • Support creators directly. We're not venture-backed and don't answer to shareholders. We stay focused on what creators actually need.

How We Approach Development

Boztik is built with a creator-first methodology. Each feature is designed to integrate naturally into professional workflows without distraction or unnecessary complexity.

Quality over speed: We release updates when they're ready, not on an arbitrary schedule. This means fewer bugs, better stability, and tools you can rely on.

Transparency: Users know what Boztik does, how it works, and why we built it this way. Documentation is comprehensive. Feature decisions are explained.

Community feedback: We actively listen to user feedback and incorporate suggestions that improve the core experience. Your creative work matters to us.
