EMAIL SUBMISSION

Tell us about you.

Use the structured submission template below to tell us about your work. The email opens with clearly separated sections and space to complete each answer. Attach your logo or profile photo before sending.

What to include
  • Business or creator name and contact name
  • Your category, short introduction and description
  • Your contact email and website
  • Your social media and Spotify links (optional)
  • Your logo and/or profile photo (optional attachments)
Open your submission template ↗

Your email app will open with the template ready. Complete the fields, attach your images, then press Send. If no email app opens, email boztiktech@gmail.com and copy the details above.

Submissions are reviewed manually. Your contact email is used for follow-up and is not displayed publicly. See our Privacy Policy.
