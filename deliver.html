The process

From completed project to your device

There are just three simple steps between receiving your link and saving your finished files.

01

Receive your link

Once the work is ready, Boztik shares your delivery URL with you directly. Keep the original message so you can return to the link while it remains active.

02

Open and review

Open the link in a modern web browser. Review the project information and available files. Supported images and documents may be previewed on the delivery page.

03

Download and save

Use the download controls on the page to save individual files or the available download bundle. Choose a folder you can find again and keep your own backup.

A few important details

Know how your delivery link works

Your link is designed for straightforward handover. These details help you use it safely and avoid losing access to your files.

Keep your link private

Deliveries are not presented as a public gallery or searchable directory. Access is provided through the unique link sent for your project.

Important: anyone who has your delivery link may be able to open it. Share it only with people you trust, and avoid posting it publicly.

Check the expiry information

Each delivery has an expiry date and time. The delivery page displays its availability information, and access is blocked once the delivery has expired.

  • Open the link as soon as you receive it.
  • Download the files you need before the displayed expiry.
  • Do not rely on the link as your long-term storage or backup.

Save a copy of your files

After downloading, confirm the files are present and open correctly on your device. Keep a second copy somewhere safe, such as an external drive or your own trusted cloud storage.

How file access is handled

The delivery page requests file access through the Boztik delivery service. The browser does not receive Boztik's underlying storage credentials or raw storage paths; file access is provided through short-lived signed links.

Help and answers

Questions about your delivery?

Here are the most common things to know when receiving finished work.

Do I need to create an account?

No account or special application is required to open a delivery link. Use the link in a web browser on a device with an internet connection.

Can I open the link on my phone?

Yes. You can open the link in a mobile browser. For larger files or multiple downloads, a computer may be more convenient and may offer more control over where files are saved.

Why can’t I open my delivery anymore?

The delivery may have reached its expiry date, or there may be a temporary connection or browser issue. Check the link and your internet connection. If the page confirms that the delivery has expired, contact Boztik and ask whether the files can be uploaded again.

What should I do if a file will not download?

Try the download again, check that your device has enough free storage and test another browser if needed. If the problem continues, contact Boztik and include the project name and a short description of the issue. Do not share your private link in a public post.

Does Boztik keep the link available forever?

No. Delivery links are time-limited. The expiry date and time shown on the delivery page determine when access ends. Download your files and keep your own backup before then.

Need a hand?

We’re here if something goes wrong.

If your delivery has expired, a download is not working or you have a question about your finished project, get in touch. Include the project name so the request can be identified more easily.
